Inside the teaching

The same mark does not mean the same thing everywhere.

A natural tooth, an implant and a removable base respond differently under load. We use the supporting anatomy to interpret what the contact may mean.

  • Paper locates contact. It does not measure force.
  • Shimstock tests whether a contact holds in that examination.
  • Movement and support determine the clinical consequence.
