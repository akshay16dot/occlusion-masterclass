What would you check next?

Clinical decisions can feel confusing when different concepts seem to point in different directions. We work through the reasoning with clinical examples.

  • A crown feels high. What do you check before adjusting?
  • How do you verify a contact?
  • When would you choose canine guidance or group function?
  • What changes for implants and complete dentures?

Why this sequence

Five connected modules

Occlusion provides the foundation. Vertical Dimension applies it through full-mouth rehabilitation (FMR) cases. The implant modules carry those restorative decisions into full-arch care.

  1. Occlusion

    Assess contacts, guidance and supporting structures. Work through checks for natural teeth, implant restorations and removable prostheses.

  2. Vertical Dimension with FMR

    Assess restorative space and apply occlusal concepts through full-mouth rehabilitation cases. FMR teaching runs across these first two modules.

  3. Implant Foundations

    Understand immediate and conventional implant approaches, patient assessment and the restorative considerations behind the plan.

  4. All-on-X Planning

    Diagnosis and planning for a fixed implant arch, with the intended restoration guiding the discussion.

  5. Surgery and Restorative Completion

    Connect the surgical workflow with restoration delivery, review and maintenance.

Questions before joining

A few useful details

When are the lectures?

The next Occlusion intake is planned for October 2026. The exact date and time, and dates for the later modules, will be confirmed before payment.

Can I take just one module?

Yes. You can enrol one module at a time and stop after the stage that meets your needs. Prerequisites still apply. For example, Vertical Dimension requires completion of our Occlusion module.

I joined the first Occlusion class. What should I book?

You qualify for Vertical Dimension with FMR. Select that individual module and let us know you attended the first Occlusion class.

Is there a separate FMR module?

No. FMR cases connect the first two modules, Occlusion and Vertical Dimension.

What happens after enrolment?

We confirm your place after payment and send joining instructions. Each module includes its recording, clinical cheat sheets and selected further reading with summaries.
